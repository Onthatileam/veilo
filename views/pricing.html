Common questions

Do my workers need to download an app?
No. Workers open a link in any browser on their phone and tap "Start Sharing." No app store, no account, no download required.
How accurate is the location tracking?
Veilo uses the device's GPS which is typically accurate to within 5–10 meters. It updates every few seconds while the worker has the page open.
Can workers turn off tracking?
Yes. Workers are always in control — tracking only runs when they actively press Start. They can stop at any time by closing the page or pressing Stop.
Can I cancel anytime?
Absolutely. No contracts, no lock-in. Cancel from your dashboard anytime and you won't be charged again.
